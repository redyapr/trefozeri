Support & Resistance Signals for Gold (XAUUSD) & Bitcoin (BTCUSD)

TREFOZERI continuously watches XAUUSD (Gold) and BTCUSD (Bitcoin) across the H1, H4 and D1 timeframes — detecting support, resistance, and SBR/RBS structure, then turning each level into a ready-to-use BUY/SELL LIMIT trade signal: an entry price, a stop loss, and several take-profit targets.

  • Multi-timeframe: S/R zones are tracked on H1, H4 and D1 in parallel, not just one timeframe.
  • Golden Zone: levels that overlap (confluence) across timeframes are flagged separately — usually a higher-probability reaction zone.
  • Automatic entry, SL & TP: every signal gets a structure-based stop loss and several take-profit levels at sensible risk/reward ratios.
  • Transparent track record: every filled signal is tracked through to close (win/loss) — see it for yourself on the Performance page, nothing is hidden.
  • Real-time alerts: new signals and price updates can be pushed straight to the Telegram channel.
